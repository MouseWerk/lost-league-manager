Quick fixes when the League client misbehaves.

Close everything

Ends every Riot and League process, for a client that is frozen or will not close.

Repair the client

Deletes the client lockfile. Helps when the client will not start, and keeps a running game open.

Clear the cache

Empties the Riot Client cache. Helps with a broken interface, endless loading and old notifications.

Game overlay Beta

A small bar over the game with your score, both teams with their ranks, and the OP.GG build for your champion. Hold Tab in the game to see the players. Show or hide it with its key (Ctrl+Shift+H at first; change it, the opacity and the lock in the overlay's own settings). The game has to run in borderless or windowed mode. The overlay is in beta and off until you switch it on.

Show the overlay

Opens the overlay while a game runs.
