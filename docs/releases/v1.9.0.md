Lost League Manager 1.9.0

## Improved

- Overview: a new page with this week's games, win rate and LP over all your accounts, a chart of
  every account's rank over the last 30 days (point at it to read each rank on that day) and a
  table with each account's week, goal progress and warnings. Click a row to open that account.
- Games and champions: click a game in the account panel, or "All games and champions", to see
  your recent games with KDA, CS, gold, damage and items, both teams of each game, and a champion
  table with games, win rate, KDA and CS per minute.
- Warnings in the account list and panel: ranked decay ("Decays in 3 days"), a ranked restriction
  with the games left, and accounts not played for 30 days. Accounts you have not signed into
  recently get an estimate for Diamond and above, marked "about".
- New sort: Last played.
- Tags: give accounts tags such as Main, Duo or Smurf in Edit (comma-separated, with suggestions
  from your other accounts). The tags show on each row and as filter chips above the list; search
  finds them too.
- Runes and items in champion select: the Live view shows the OP.GG runes, items and skill order
  for the champion you pick, for your position (or another one you choose), and imports the rune
  page and an item set into the client with one click. Your own rune pages are only replaced
  after you confirm. Optional: import both automatically when you lock in (Settings > Automation).
- The in-game overlay is here: switch it on in Settings > Game overlay. It shows your score, the
  ranks of all ten players and your item build over the game, keeps the place you drag it to,
  and works with or without Overwolf's game events.
- Layout: choose Roomy in Settings > Appearance for the larger buttons, rows and text of 1.7, on
  every page and in every dialog. Compact stays the default.

## Fixed

- One-click login: after signing in, the Riot Client often stayed on its home page and League only
  started after a long wait or not at all. The app now starts League through the Riot Client as
  soon as the sign-in is done.
- The interface size buttons could also switch other buttons of the same look in Settings.
- Overlay: player names from the game are shown as text, never as markup; the opacity was
  applied twice; any key combination could be set as its hotkey.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Play an account with one click: right after the Riot Client sign-in, League starts by itself
  and logs in, without clicking Play in the Riot Client. With a verification code, League starts
  only after the code is entered.
- Settings > Appearance > Layout > Roomy: buttons, rows and text get larger on Accounts,
  Overview, Live, Tools, Stat viewer, Settings and in the dialogs; Compact brings back 1.8.
- Give two accounts the tag "Duo" in Edit: a "Duo 2" chip appears above the list; clicking it shows
  only those two; clicking it again shows all. The order cannot be dragged while it is on.
- Open Overview after the app has looked up ranks for a while: the tiles, chart and table show
  your accounts; pointing at the chart lists each rank on that day.
- Open an account, click one of the game squares: the games dialog opens on that game and loads
  both teams; the Champions tab lists your champions.
- Sign into a Diamond account that is close to decay: the row shows the hourglass and the panel
  says "Decays in N days". (Needs the real client: the decay fields come from it.)
- Start a custom game or practice tool, open Live in champion select, pick a champion: the runes
  and items appear; Import runes adds "LLM <Champion> <Position>" to the rune pages; Import item
  set adds it in the shop. With all rune pages in use, the app asks before replacing the current
  one.
- Turn on "Import runes and items" in Settings > Automation, lock in a champion: both are imported
  once, with a message.
- Turn on the Game overlay, start a practice tool game in borderless mode: the overlay appears in
  the top right with your score; Ctrl+Shift+H hides and shows it; drag it, restart the app, the
  next game shows it at the same place. Turn it off: it is gone.
