Lost League Manager 1.11.0

## Improved

- Sign in without typing: click Sign in in an account's panel, sign in to that account in the Riot
  Client yourself and tick "Stay signed in". From then on Play signs the account in by itself,
  without typing the password and without taking over mouse and keyboard. The sign-in is kept
  encrypted on this PC; if it expires, Play types the password as before. What's new explains the
  steps on the first start of this version.
- The app starts faster: the window opens in about half the time, and the icons no longer load from
  the internet.
- The account list shows more: LP and win rate under the rank, the flex rank of accounts without a
  solo rank, when each account last played and its Blue Essence.
- Every game mode has its name in the Live view, the games list and today's session: ARAM Mayhem,
  Arena, Swarm, Brawl, Swiftplay and more. Champion select in ARAM Mayhem shows the ARAM runes.
- Blue Essence and RP show the League client's own currency icons.
- The account tools in the panel (Login, Password, OP.GG, Sign in, Edit, Delete) are an even grid.

## Fixed

- Buttons in the account panel show their hover in the dark theme.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Start 1.11.0: What's new opens with the steps for signing in without typing.
- Open an account, click Sign in, sign in in the Riot Client with "Stay signed in" ticked: League
  starts, and the panel says "Signs in without typing".
- Play another account, then this one again: it signs in without any typing.
- The account list shows LP and win rate, "Played ... ago" and Blue Essence (when the window is
  wide enough).
- Play an ARAM Mayhem game: the lobby, queue and games list say "ARAM Mayhem"; champion select shows
  ARAM runes.
- Dark theme: hovering the panel's tools changes their background.
- Without internet: icons still show.
