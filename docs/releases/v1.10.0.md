Lost League Manager 1.10.0

## Improved

- When the League client is signed in with an account that is not saved in the app, the account
  list offers to add it, with its Riot ID and server filled in; only the password is left to type.
- Riot ID changes are followed: when you sign in with an account whose Riot ID changed, or (with a
  Riot API key) when a saved Riot ID is no longer found, the account gets its new Riot ID and keeps
  its rank history and saved games.
- The account panel shows the Blue Essence and RP of the account as the client showed them at its
  last sign-in.
- Select several accounts (Select next to the search) to add a tag, refresh their ranks, export
  them to a backup or delete them together.
- Today's session in the Live view: games since midnight with their champions, wins and losses,
  today's LP and the current streak. After two losses in a row it suggests a short break.
- Season review for each account (Season review in the panel): peak rank, current rank, the rank
  curve of the season, the season record and most played champions from OP.GG, and the longest
  ranked win streak in the saved games.
- Matchups tab in the games dialog: your record against each lane opponent, and which champions
  you played into them.
- Champion select: pick an enemy (your lane opponent first, when the game shows lanes) to see the
  OP.GG matchup: win rate, the runes, items, summoner spells and skill order that do best in it,
  and your own record against that champion. With a friend in your party, it shows how well your
  two champions do together.
- Discord status: your solo rank with its emblem, and in champion select and in a game your
  champion and queue. Settings > App behavior > "Rank and champion in Discord" turns this off;
  account names are never shown.

## Fixed

- The selection bar, season review and games dialog fit the Roomy layout.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Sign the League client into an account that is not saved in the app: the account list offers to
  add it; Add account opens the form with the login name, Riot ID and server filled in.
- Sign in with an account, open it: the panel shows its Blue Essence and RP.
- Click Select, pick two accounts, Add tag "Test": both rows show the tag. Refresh ranks updates
  both; Export asks for a passphrase and saves only those two.
- Play a game, open Live: Today shows the game, the record and the LP.
- Open an account, Season review: peak and current rank, the curve and the longest win streak.
- Open an account's games, Matchups: lane opponents with your record (Summoner's Rift games).
- In a ranked champion select, pick a champion: the enemy picks appear under Matchup; clicking one
  shows the OP.GG matchup. In a duo, the duo line shows your synergy.
- With Discord open: the status shows your rank, and in a game your champion and queue. Turn off
  "Rank and champion in Discord": only the phase is left.
