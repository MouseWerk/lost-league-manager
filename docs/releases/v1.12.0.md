Lost League Manager 1.12.0

## Improved

- Suspended accounts show how long the suspension still runs: "Banned 1d 6h" in the account list
  and the exact end in the panel, counting down while the app is open. The app reads the ban from
  the Riot Client when it signs the account in, and stops the launch instead of opening League's
  ban screen. A suspension can also be entered by hand in the account form ("Suspended until").
- The Overview is new: the week of all accounts in one band (ranked games, win rate, LP and the
  Blue Essence of every account), a card per account with its rank, the last 30 days as a line,
  the week and the goal, and a "Needs attention" list with bans, decay, restrictions and inactive
  accounts. Click a card or an entry to open the account.
- In-game overlay (beta): a new Items panel shows the OP.GG core build with its win rate, the
  boots, and the choices for the 4th, 5th and 6th item with how often each is picked and won.
  Items you already own are ticked. The overlay is a little wider so its bar has room.

## Fixed

- Dates, numbers and the date picker are in English like the rest of the app, whatever language
  Windows uses.
- In select mode, only the picked accounts are highlighted.
- The games dialog of an account without saved games shows one clear message.
- Narrow windows: the account rows and the rank history in the panel no longer cut text off, and
  the Overview shows what needs attention first.
- The Overview no longer shows an old LP value next to "Unranked".
- The season review says "No ranked games saved yet" instead of "in 0 saved ranked games".

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Start 1.12.0: What's new lists the changes.
- Edit an account, set "Suspended until" to tomorrow: the list shows "Banned" with the time left,
  the panel the exact end; after a minute the time left goes down. Clear the field again.
- With a suspended account (if you have one): Play stops with "suspended until ...".
- Open Overview: the summary band, a card per account and Needs attention. Click a card: the
  account opens in Accounts.
- In a game with the overlay on: the bag button opens Items with the core build and the choices;
  bought items are ticked.
- Dates read in English (for example "Nov 4, 9:59 PM").
