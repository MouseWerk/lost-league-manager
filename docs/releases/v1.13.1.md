Lost League Manager 1.13.1

## Improved

- Stay signed in (#13): when Play types the password, the app now ticks "Stay signed in" in the Riot
  Client. The sign-in is kept, so the next launch of that account signs in without typing. On by
  default; turn it off in Settings > League client to log in as before.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Start 1.13.1: What's new lists the change.
- Settings > League client: "Stay signed in" is on.
- Play an account without a saved sign-in: "Stay signed in" is ticked in the Riot Client before the
  username is typed, and League starts.
- Close everything and Play the same account again: it signs in without typing.
- Turn the setting off and Play another account: the box is left as it is.
